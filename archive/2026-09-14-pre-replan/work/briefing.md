# Briefing (built)

Spoken turn: `brief me` / `catch me up` / `what's on`.

**Speak:** one or two sentences. Who needs Tony + what’s next on the clock. Unread *count* only if it actually matters (not every morning).

**Board:** priority mail only (from + subject). No Unread/Today KPIs, no timeline, no dropped files, no session memory.

**Priority mail:** people at work — named humans / real threads (Deepak, Neha, …). Skip banks, LinkedIn, Amazon, and similar.

**Calendar:** still *read* so speak can say the next event. Not drawn on this board.

Same pattern as research/mail: tools fetch unread + next event; Gemini writes speak/board from those notes; do not invent senders or meetings.
