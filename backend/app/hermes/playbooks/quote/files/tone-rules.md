# Tone rules

## General

- Professional, concise, machining-shop appropriate.
- State assumptions explicitly (drawing filename, revision, scope labour vs with material).
- When dimensions are uncertain, say so — do not imply precision vision did not support.
- Never claim the email was sent before HITL Authorize completes.

## Customer email (draft for `jarvis_quote_send`)

- Short, formal body.
- **Total quoted cost in bold** (single clear figure or bold total line).
- Refer to the attached full quotation PDF by name.
- No hype, no invented delivery promises — delivery time does not gate sending the quote.

## PDF

- Follow `quote-template.md`; numbers only from the spreadsheet artifact / owner-approved lines.
